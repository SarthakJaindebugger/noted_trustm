Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for daycare in Espoo?
You can apply online via the Espoo city website or visit the Service Square. Our team can help you with the application during your next appointment.
What documents do I need for integration services?
You need your residence permit, passport, and any previous education certificates. If you have dependents, bring their documents as well.
Is there free language training?
Yes, Espoo offers free Finnish and Swedish courses for immigrants. You can enroll through the integration program.
How can I contact my case officer?
You can send a message through this dashboard (Messages tab) or call the Service Square. Your officer's contact details are in your profile.
What childcare options are available for shift workers?
Espoo offers extended-hour daycare in some locations. Please discuss your work schedule with your advisor to find a suitable place.
